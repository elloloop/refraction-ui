Add the Loader family: `Spinner` (ring, dots, bars, orbit, ripple), `LoadingBar` / `PageLoadingBar` for route transitions, `LoadingOverlay`, and the `useDelayedLoading` / `useTrickleProgress` hooks. All loaders are polite live regions, take colour from theme tokens, and collapse to a slow pulse under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
