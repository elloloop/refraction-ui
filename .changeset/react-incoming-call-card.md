Add `IncomingCallCard` to `@refraction-ui/react`: a ringing audio or video call as an `alertdialog` with caller avatar and name, optional context, Accept and Decline, an optional self-running or app-driven countdown (`timeoutSeconds`, `remaining`, `onTimeout`), configurable Esc (`escapeBehavior`), and `overlay` or `window` layouts. Focus lands on the card, not Accept, so a stray Enter cannot answer. (#588)
