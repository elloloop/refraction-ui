Add `VideoStage`, the stateful stage of a video call, built on `VideoTile`. It shows a grid that pages past four tiles (two rows that scroll sideways on a landscape stage, two columns that scroll down on a portrait one, with previous and next buttons and a scroll thumb), and a presentation layout with one participant on the stage and the rest in a rail beside it or, on a portrait or narrow (under 600 px) stage, below it. Each tile's options pin it to the stage ("Spotlight") or maximize it over the whole stage, and `renderTileActions` adds your own actions. A pin beats a shared screen, which beats the active speaker; a screen share that starts during the call is pinned, and `autoSpotlightSpeaker` puts the speaker on the stage until someone else speaks. Pin and zoom can be controlled or uncontrolled. Media comes in through `renderMedia`, so the stage works with any call SDK. Escape closes the options and leaves the maximized view, focus follows the participant after each action, the layout under a maximized tile is inert, and paging respects reduced motion. The layout and selection rules are a headless core with their own tests.
