Add `PinnedBar` to `@refraction-ui/react`: the pinned-items strip above a conversation. It shows the current pin's author, a one-line snippet and its position ("2 of 5") inside a labelled `role="region"`, with wrapping previous/next, optional `onOpen`, `onOpenList` and `onUnpin` controls, a collapse toggle (`collapsed`/`defaultCollapsed`/`onCollapsedChange`), overridable `labels`, and polite live-region announcements when the current pin changes. (#582)
